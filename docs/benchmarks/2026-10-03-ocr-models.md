## Decision

`DEFAULT_CONFIG = { det: 'v6-tiny', rec: 'v6-tiny', longSide: 960 }`. Chosen by the user on 2026-10-03 for speed: the
v6-tiny pair reads ≥ 90% of phrases (spec success criterion 5) at roughly a third of the scan time and download
of the v5 and v6-small pairs. It misreads a few rare characters (粿 → 棵, 嬷 → 嫉). The selection rule (spec §6.7:
≥ 90% exact, then fastest, then smallest download) reproduces the choice.

`v6-small` is also deployed, so the trade-off can be checked on a phone with `?det=v6-small&rec=v6-small`.
Supersedes `docs/benchmarks/2026-10-02-ocr-models.md`, which measured the KKM photo only.
