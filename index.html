PinyinLens

Point your camera at Chinese text, freeze it, and read it with pinyin.

  1. Aim at a menu or sign. Pinch to zoom in on small text.
  2. Tap the shutter to freeze and scan.
  3. Pinch and drag to look around; tap again to go back to the camera.

The first visit downloads the reading engine once (up to 20 MB). After that it works offline.
